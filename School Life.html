School Life at Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School
Vibrant, Engaging, and Inspiring

Daily Schedule

Our school day is structured to balance academics, physical activity, and personal development. The schedule promotes a healthy routine while allowing time for creativity and rest.

Time Activity
8:00 AM - 8:30 AM Assembly & Morning Prayer
8:30 AM - 12:00 PM Academic Classes
12:00 PM - 12:30 PM Lunch Break
12:30 PM - 2:00 PM Academic Classes & Activities
2:00 PM - 2:30 PM Recess & Free Play
2:30 PM - 3:30 PM Co-Curricular Sessions
3:30 PM School Dismissal

Note: Schedule may vary for different grade levels. After-school programs available until 5:00 PM.

Co-Curricular Activities

Beyond academics, we offer a rich array of co-curricular activities to nurture talents and interests. These programs help students develop well-rounded personalities and life skills.

  • Art Club: Painting, drawing, and crafts
  • Music Society: Vocal and instrumental training
  • Drama Theater: Acting and public speaking
  • Debate Team: Critical thinking and oratory skills
  • Science Club: Experiments and innovation projects
  • Literary Circle: Reading, writing, and poetry
  • Environmental Group: Sustainability initiatives

Activities are held weekly, with opportunities to participate in inter-school competitions and exhibitions.

Sports & Games

Physical fitness is integral to our school life. We promote sports as a means of building teamwork, discipline, and healthy lifestyles.

  • Cricket: Team practices and matches
  • Football: Skill development and tournaments
  • Badminton: Indoor court facilities
  • Table Tennis: Recreational and competitive play
  • Athletics: Track and field events
  • Yoga & Fitness: Daily wellness sessions
  • Indoor Games: Chess, carrom, and board games

Our sports program includes professional coaching, annual sports day, and participation in district-level competitions.

School Events (Annual Day)

Throughout the year, we host exciting events that bring our community together and showcase student talents. These occasions create lasting memories and foster school spirit.

  • Annual Day: Cultural performances and awards ceremony
  • Science Fair: Student projects and demonstrations
  • Sports Day: Athletic competitions and fun games
  • Independence Day: Patriotic programs and parades
  • Eid Celebrations: Cultural activities and feasts
  • Art Exhibition: Display of student artwork
  • Field Trips: Educational outings to museums and parks

Our Annual Day is the highlight, featuring spectacular shows prepared over months of rehearsal.

Uniform & Discipline

We believe in fostering a disciplined environment that promotes respect and responsibility. Our uniform policy and code of conduct support this goal.

Uniform Guidelines

  • Boys: White shirt, navy blue trousers, school tie, black shoes
  • Girls: White shalwar kameez with navy blue dupatta, black shoes
  • Sports Uniform: School tracksuit for PE days
  • Winter: Navy blue sweater or blazer
  • Accessories: School badge, ID card required daily

Discipline Policy

Our progressive discipline approach focuses on positive reinforcement and learning from mistakes. Rules include punctuality, respect for others, no bullying, and proper use of school property.

Parent-Teacher Association

The PTA plays a vital role in bridging home and school, fostering partnership for student success.

  • Regular meetings to discuss school matters
  • Volunteer opportunities for events and activities
  • Workshops on parenting and child development
  • Fundraising for school improvements
  • Feedback channels for continuous enhancement

Join the PTA to actively contribute to your child's educational journey and the school community.

Notice of Nondiscriminatory Policy as to Students

Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School welcomes students from all backgrounds and provides equal opportunities to every child. The school does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, language, nationality, or social background in the administration of its educational programs, admissions, or activities. Every student is entitled to the same rights, respect, and learning opportunities within our inclusive environment.
