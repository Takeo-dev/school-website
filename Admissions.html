Admissions Overview

Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School welcomes applications from families who value quality education in a nurturing environment. Our admissions process is designed to be transparent and supportive, ensuring the best fit for both the student and the school.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nursery: Children must be 3 years old by March 31 of the admission year.
  • Primary (Grades 1-5): Previous school records and an entrance test may be required.
  • Middle School (Grades 6-8): Academic performance review and interview.
  • Open to all nationalities, with no discrimination based on race, religion, or background.
  • Priority given to siblings of current students and children of alumni.

For any questions, contact our admissions office at admissions@ahmedanschool.edu or +92-XXX-XXXXXXX.

Admission Process

Our admission process is straightforward and family-friendly. Follow these steps to join our school community.

Step 1: Inquiry

Contact us via phone, email, or visit our campus for an initial consultation.

Step 2: Application

Submit the online application form with required documents.

Step 3: Assessment

Students may need to take an entrance test or attend an interview.

Step 4: Offer & Enrollment

Receive an admission offer and complete enrollment by paying fees.

Step 5: Orientation

Attend our welcome orientation for new families.

Fees are payable quarterly. Scholarships available for deserving students. Contact us for details.

Required Documents

To complete your application, please submit the following documents:

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate of the child
  • Previous school records/transcripts
  • Passport-sized photographs (child and parents)
  • Copy of parent's CNIC or passport
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Proof of residence

All documents must be submitted in original and photocopy format during the admission process.

Notice of Nondiscriminatory Policy as to Students

Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School welcomes students from all backgrounds and provides equal opportunities to every child. The school does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, language, nationality, or social background in the administration of its educational programs, admissions, or activities. Every student is entitled to the same rights, respect, and learning opportunities within our inclusive environment.
