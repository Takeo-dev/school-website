Academics at Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School
Building Foundations for Success

Curriculum Overview

At Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School, our curriculum is designed to foster holistic development, combining academic rigor with character building. Aligned with national standards and incorporating global best practices, it emphasizes critical thinking, creativity, and ethical values from Nursery to Grade 8.

Our approach integrates modern teaching methods with traditional values, preparing students for future challenges while nurturing their individual talents. The curriculum is regularly updated to include emerging technologies and contemporary issues.

  • Balanced blend of core subjects and co-curricular activities
  • Focus on STEM education alongside arts and humanities
  • Emphasis on language skills and cultural awareness
  • Incorporation of life skills and character education
  • Progressive learning from foundational to advanced concepts

Primary I (Grades 1-3)

In the early primary years, we focus on building strong foundations in basic skills while making learning fun and engaging. Our program encourages curiosity, creativity, and confidence through play-based and interactive activities.

  • Development of reading, writing, and numeracy skills
  • Introduction to science and social studies through hands-on experiments
  • Basic computer literacy and digital awareness
  • Art, music, and physical education for holistic growth
  • Emphasis on social-emotional learning and values education

Classes are kept small to ensure personalized attention, with a student-teacher ratio of 20:1. Assessment is continuous and formative, focusing on progress rather than competition.

Primary II (Grades 4-5)

As students progress to upper primary, we build on the foundations laid earlier, introducing more structured learning while maintaining an element of discovery. This stage prepares students for middle school by developing independent thinking and study skills.

  • Advanced language arts with reading comprehension and creative writing
  • Mathematics including basic geometry and problem-solving
  • Science with simple experiments and environmental awareness
  • Social studies covering history, geography, and civics
  • Introduction to foreign languages and advanced computer skills

We incorporate project-based learning and group activities to foster teamwork and leadership skills. Regular field trips and guest lectures enrich the curriculum.

Subjects Taught

Our comprehensive curriculum covers a wide range of subjects to provide a well-rounded education. Core subjects are taught across all grades, with electives introduced in higher levels.

  • English: Language arts, literature, and communication skills
  • Mathematics: Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, and logical reasoning
  • Science: Physics, chemistry, biology, and environmental science
  • Social Studies: History, geography, civics, and economics
  • Languages: Urdu, Sindhi, and introductory Arabic or French
  • Computer Science: Basic programming, digital literacy, and internet safety
  • Arts: Visual arts, music, and performing arts
  • Physical Education: Sports, yoga, and health education
  • Islamic Studies/Ethics: Moral values and character development

Subjects are integrated where possible to show real-world connections, enhancing student engagement and understanding.

Teaching Methodology

Our teaching methodology is student-centered, blending traditional and modern approaches to cater to diverse learning styles. We employ a mix of instructional strategies to ensure effective learning for all students.

  • Interactive lectures and discussions for conceptual understanding
  • Hands-on experiments and projects for practical application
  • Technology integration with smart boards and educational software
  • Collaborative learning through group activities and peer teaching
  • Differentiated instruction to address individual needs
  • Formative assessments and regular feedback for continuous improvement
  • Inquiry-based learning to foster critical thinking
  • Integration of real-world examples and case studies

Our teachers undergo regular professional development to stay updated with the latest educational practices. We also encourage parental involvement in the learning process.

Notice of Nondiscriminatory Policy as to Students

Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School welcomes students from all backgrounds and provides equal opportunities to every child. The school does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, language, nationality, or social background in the administration of its educational programs, admissions, or activities. Every student is entitled to the same rights, respect, and learning opportunities within our inclusive environment.
