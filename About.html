Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a nurturing environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and morally. Our holistic approach ensures that every child receives a well-rounded education.

Mission Statement

To provide quality education that nurtures intellectual curiosity, moral integrity, and social responsibility, preparing students to become compassionate leaders and lifelong learners.

Vision Statement

To be a leading educational institution recognized for excellence in holistic education, innovation, and community engagement, empowering students to excel in a global society.

Our History

Founded in 1990 in Shikarpur, Sindh, Pakistan, Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School was established to offer a blend of modern and traditional education, drawing inspiration from the legacy of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan's educational reforms.

1990: Founding

The school was founded as a primary institution with a mission to provide accessible education to local children.

2000: Expansion to Middle School

Expanded facilities and curriculum to include middle school grades, introducing more subjects and extracurricular activities.

2010: Introduction of Grade 8

Added higher grades and modernized infrastructure, including science labs and computer facilities.

2020: Digital Transformation

Adopted online learning tools and enhanced teacher training during the global pandemic.

2025: 35th Anniversary

Celebrating 35 years of excellence with new initiatives for student development.

  • Science Laboratories: Fully equipped labs for physics, chemistry, and biology experiments.

Notice of Nondiscriminatory Policy as to Students

Ahmedan Anglo Oriental School welcomes students from all backgrounds and provides equal opportunities to every child. The school does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, language, nationality, or social background in the administration of its educational programs, admissions, or activities. Every student is entitled to the same rights, respect, and learning opportunities within our inclusive environment.
